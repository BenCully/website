What it means for buyers

  • Repos flow through wholesale auctions first, so supply shows up at dealers with a lag of months — watch the Manheim index for softening. So far it hasn't: the mid-October reading rose 0.7% to 207.4, and Cox expects wholesale prices to end 2026 only about +0.2% YoY (Cox Q3 release, Oct 7).
  • Where softness is showing: Cox says pickups remain soft and large pickups/SUVs are absorbing the fuel-cost hit, while compact cars are up 5.6% YoY. Retail used inventory rose to 49 days' supply in September, with cars under $15k at just 34 days — cheap cars are still scarce.
  • More supply is coming off lease: Cox expects off-lease volume to rise from about 190,000 a month in the first half of 2026 to about 220,000 a month in the second half, and negative equity means more of those cars go to auction instead of being bought out (Cox Q3 2026 presentation).
  • Lenders are feeling it: in Q3 2026 GM Financial's loan-loss provision rose to $340M from $244M, Ally's retail auto net charge-offs were 1.89%, and subprime lender Credit Acceptance swung to a $56.6M GAAP loss as loan performance worsened. Rising lender losses usually mean more repossessed cars at auction in the months ahead.
  • Most likely bargains: 2021–2023 model-year compacts, crossovers and full-size trucks bought at peak prices.
  • Low subprime recovery (38%) means lenders are dumping cars cheaply at auction — dealers may capture that, not you; negotiate.
  • Caution: Philadelphia Fed research suggests delinquent loans are staying unresolved longer rather than surging, so a sudden "flood" isn't guaranteed [2]; the American Recovery Association likewise says lenders are holding delinquent accounts 90–120+ days before repossessing, and Manheim repo-volume growth has slowed to 9% (Auto Wire, Oct 21 2026). Fitch notes tax refunds boosted used-vehicle demand and recoveries in spring 2026 before fading into summer [3].

Forecast formula

Formula: P(t) = P0 × (1 + d + m + f)^t, with t = months from Oct 2026. P0 = CarEdge's current average used price for the 3-year-old 2023 model year (2024 for the Trax, which has no 2023 entry; the Envista has no CarEdge price, so it isn't forecast). d = monthly aging depreciation = (1 − a)^(1/12) − 1, where a = 1 − (1 − dep5)^(1/5), from CarEdge's 5-yr depreciation. m = market drift = (MUVVI Sep 26 ÷ Sep 25)^(1/12) − 1 = (205.88/207.03)^(1/12) − 1 = -0.046%/mo (the mid-Oct 2026 checkpoint is not used until Cox publishes the official October value). f = flood adjustment = −0.2%/mo × (score − 50)/50, an assumption of about ±2.4%/yr at the extremes. Band = base × (1 ± 1.2816·σ·√t), where σ = 1.32% is the standard deviation of monthly MUVVI changes from Sep 2023 to Sep 2026 (roughly an 80% band). These are wholesale-index-driven retail estimates for a car that keeps aging, not price quotes.

Method & verification

Flood score formula

Scoring formula (estimate): Score = 100 × (0.40·D + 0.25·S + 0.20·R + 0.15·A)
D = min-max normalized CarEdge 5-yr depreciation % (model-level). S = normalized Cox new-vehicle days' supply: model-level where Cox published it (Ram 1500, Grand Cherokee, Wrangler, RAV4, Camry), otherwise brand-level (Sep 2026, incl. Lincoln 127 and Mini 132 via Ford Authority and Chevrolet 88 via GM Authority; Nissan Aug, Kia Jun 2026); brands without a published figure (Mitsubishi, Hyundai, VW, Mercedes, BMW, Audi, Tesla, Mazda, Infiniti, Alfa, Chrysler) use the industry's 82 days, flagged. R = 1 if LoanTape's subprime bond data flags the make as worse after age adjustment (Mitsubishi, Dodge, Kia, Jeep, Nissan) or lowest raw recovery (Mercedes, BMW, Audi), else 0; it's qualitative because LoanTape has an open data-correction notice. A = affordability proxy (1 − normalized newest-model-year new price, CarEdge). This assumes cheaper cars have more subprime buyers; Experian shows subprime is 20.6% of used and 6.88% of new financing (Q1 2026) but has no figures by model. Price direction: ↓↓ score ≥60, ↓ 45–59, → below 45. No per-model repossession, loan APR/term, or incentive data was publicly available, so they aren't inputs.
